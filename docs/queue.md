## 1. Sound

```
SOUND (owner, 11 Nov). Start with git pull. Campus and SunnyBay must
never be silent.
1. Ambience zones: beach waves and gulls at the Beachfront, street
   hum and the odd hooter in Town centre, wind in the Suburbs, a
   murmur of voices inside places, the existing crowd sound in the
   Caf. Each fades as you cross a border.
2. Music: the existing tracks (public/audio) placed by zone and time
   of day, quiet under the ambience, with the music switch working.
3. Spot sounds: the bus engine when a taxi arrives, doors on every
   place door, footsteps, the chat ping kept.
4. iPhone: unlock audio on the first tap. Load each zone's files
   only when needed. New audio under 1.5 MB total; any sourced file
   must be CC0 or ours, listed in public/audio/CREDITS.md.
5. Tests: no console errors, the sound switch silences everything,
   zone changes at a border. Quick project green. Push and deploy.
```

## 2. Movement

```
MOVEMENT (owner, 11 Nov). Start with git pull. SunnyBay must look
busy.
1. KOMBIS: minibus taxis drive the roads between stops. Taking a
   taxi: one pulls up, you board, it pulls off with the camera
   following for 3 to 5 seconds, then cut to it pulling in at your
   stop and you stepping out. The fare and time rules stay.
2. TRAFFIC: 8 to 15 cars at a time on the main roads and the ring,
   slowing at crossings. Cars slow and stop for the player and for
   pedestrians: nobody gets hit yet (the injury loop comes later).
3. PEOPLE: pedestrians crossing at crossings; patrons in places sit,
   browse, queue and leave on a loop instead of standing still.
4. Performance: instanced meshes; every district holds 30 fps at 4x
   throttle. Report the numbers.
5. Tests, quick project green, screenshots, push and deploy.
```
