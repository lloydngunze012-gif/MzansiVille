# Launch kit

Design for Claude to review before Claude Code builds it. Not wired. Testers arrive Wednesday 11 Nov. Public launch is Friday 13 Nov. The campus sits in SunnyBay.

## Tester briefing

### WhatsApp message

Hey. You're on the Soft Life tester list. Welcome to SunnyBay.

The campus is the University of Mzansi. Play on your phone. Do the quick start first, then take a taxi, wave at someone, and eat at the Caf. That is the whole first hour.

If the game trips, screenshot it and tap Feedback. Tell us what you tapped and what you expected. We read every one.

Public launch is Friday 13 Nov. Until then, this is your campus. Bring a friend if you want. Sharp.

### Checklist

Tick these on the first sit-down.

- [ ] Finish the quick start on your phone
- [ ] Take a minibus taxi somewhere in SunnyBay
- [ ] Wave at someone who waves back
- [ ] Queue at the Caf and eat
- [ ] Open Things to do at two different places (campus places count)
- [ ] Work one paid gig
- [ ] Sit and study at the library
- [ ] Run the race to the pier, win or lose
- [ ] Stay until Night and put the day to bed
- [ ] Send one Feedback note, even if it is "all good"

### How to report a bug

1. Screenshot the screen while it is still wrong. Do not close the card first.
2. Tap **Feedback**.
3. Write three lines: what you tapped, what you saw, what you expected.
4. Add the screenshot. Phone model and the time of day in the game help, if you have them.
5. Send. Do not wait for a reply in the chat. If it happens again, send again with a new screenshot.

## Weekly challenges

Playable from the first weeks. Steps use only gigs, taxis, the Caf, the library, the gym, waving, the race to the pier, and saving money. Rags to Riches also uses the driving school. Rewards are a badge plus game rand.

### 1. Rags to Riches

Start from R0 and buy the first car. This one can run across the first weeks.

1. Accept the challenge: your wallet resets to R0.
2. Work a gig every day. Do not skip a shift.
3. Eat at the Caf, the cheap plate, nothing extra.
4. Save R5,350 (licence plus first car).
5. Get your licence at the driving school (R850).
6. Buy the first car.

**Reward:** Empty Pockets badge + R300

### 2. Pier Pressure

1. Warm up at the gym.
2. Taxi down toward the water.
3. Wave at someone on the way.
4. Run the race to the pier.
5. Win or lose, taxi home and save whatever is left.

**Reward:** Salt Shoes badge + R150

### 3. Tray Service

1. Eat at the Caf three different days.
2. Wave at someone in the queue.
3. Work a Caf-day gig after a meal.
4. Save R200 from that week without touching it.

**Reward:** Tray Regular badge + R200

### 4. Quiet Money

1. Study at the campus library twice.
2. Taxi once to the SunnyBay Public Library.
3. Eat once at the Caf, not twice in a day.
4. End the week with more money than you started it.

**Reward:** Page Saver badge + R180

### 5. Five Hellos

1. Wave at five different people.
2. Take a taxi with at least one of those days.
3. Sit at the Caf after a wave, not before.
4. Save R100 you would have spent on a second plate.

**Reward:** Soft Wave badge + R100

### 6. Rank Map

1. Take three taxis to three different places.
2. Wave at someone at a taxi stop.
3. One of the trips must end near the pier.
4. Do not race. Just arrive, then taxi back.
5. Save the fare you did not spend on a fourth trip.

**Reward:** Forward Fare badge + R120

### 7. Shift Clock

1. Work four gigs in one week.
2. Eat at the Caf only on gig days.
3. Gym once, so the Energy bill does not pile up.
4. Put every pay packet into savings before you spend.

**Reward:** Shift Clock badge + R250

### 8. Two Thousand One Hundred

Play the first week on the R2,100 you woke up with.

1. Taxi only twice.
2. Caf for food, not for showing off.
3. One library sit, one gym sit.
4. Wave at your roommate's friends, not only strangers.
5. Finish the week with money still in the pocket.

**Reward:** Starter Float badge + R210
