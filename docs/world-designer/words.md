# Launch words

Design for Claude to review before Claude Code builds it. Not wired. Tone: warm, funny, South African English, for everyone. The campus sits in SunnyBay.

## Billboards

Roadside boards in the city. Max 7 words.

### Woza

- Pay. Split. Woza. Done.
- One tap. Taxi sorted.
- Money home before supper.

### Lifecup

- Stay. The WiFi is honest.
- Flat white. Quiet corner. Repeat.
- Lifecup already knows yours.

### Greenway

- Hot bread. Cold juice. Sorted.
- Stock the house in one.
- Greenway fills the fridge.

### University of Mzansi

- University of Mzansi. Start here.
- Seven houses. One campus. Yours.
- Walk in. Soft Life starts.
