# City places

Design for Claude to review before Claude Code builds it. Not wired. This file is the Things to do list for every place off the campus.

The campus sits in the middle of the city. A player walks, takes a minibus taxi, or drives. Inside a place they open Things to do and pick one row.

Wave 1 is in the game at launch (18 places). Wave 2 opens after launch (12 places).

## How to read a row

Day slots, in order: Morning, Lecture, Social, Free, Night. The Open line is when the door is open. Any row at that place can be picked in an open slot.

Minutes are how long the row takes. Quick is Yes from 15 to 30 minutes, and No outside that.

Cost is what leaves the pocket, in game rand. A paid gig costs R0 and puts its pay in Money.

The stats column is Money, Energy, Fullness, Grades, Social, in that order. `0` means that stat stays still.

On a row marked "Better with friends here", the Social number is the one you get with friends in the place. Alone, Social is half, rounded down. The row still runs.

A gig costs Energy. Pays sit between R120 and R240. The best gig pays exactly twice the smallest. No gig pays more than that.

The driving school's licence test grants the driver's licence. A row that needs the licence says so. Buying the used car needs the licence. A row that needs a car of your own says so.

The lounge is music, a pool table, board games, karaoke, milkshakes and mocktails. Arcade wins pay out as cheers and a printed photo, never as cash.

Brands that may appear: Greenway, Lifecup, Woza. No others.

## Money check

Written prices, kept as given:

- A new player starts on R2,100.
- A meal in these lists is R30 to R80. Snacks can be less. A show-off spread can be more, because it is the flex.
- A taxi trip is R15 to R25. Chartering a whole van is a show-off, not a trip.
- The first car is R4,500. The used runabout is R12,000. The licence comes first.
- Gigs pay R120 to R240 a shift. R240 is exactly twice R120.

A game week here is 7 days. Light living, used only for this check, is one R40 meal and one R20 taxi a day: R60, or R420 across a full week.

The best gig is stewarding at the stadium, R240. Worked every day that is R1,680. After light living, that is R1,260 saved in a week. An average gig is about R160, or R1,120 a week, which nets R700 after living. The smallest gig, folding at the laundromat, is R120, or R840 if it is worked every day.

**Week 1 goal: the driver's licence (R850).** The driving school is in Wave 1, so the licence is available from launch. The path is the yard lesson (R180), the road lesson (R220) and the test (R450). From R2,100 most players get it in week 1 and keep about R1,250.

**First car, R4,500.** Needs the licence. Best gig every day (R240) with light living (R60 a day):

- End of week 1, after the licence and living: R2,100 + R1,680 − R420 − R850 = R2,510.
- End of week 2: R2,510 + R1,260 = R3,770.
- End of week 3: R3,770 + R1,260 = R5,030.

R5,030 covers the R4,500 first car.

An average gig (about R160) nets R700 a week after living:

- End of week 1, after the licence: R2,100 + R1,120 − R420 − R850 = R1,950.
- End of week 5: R1,950 + (R700 × 4) = R4,750.

Around week 5 the first car is in reach.

**Used runabout, R12,000.** A long goal. On the best gig every day with light living, from R2,100: end of week 7 is R2,100 + (R1,260 × 7) = R10,920; end of week 8 is R12,180. About week 8.

Week-1 flex after the licence still fits the show-offs near R1,000 to R2,000 (the Lifecup tab, the year of laundry, the deluxe tank, the chartered van, the beach cabana), plus the R800 student phone. The flagship, the season seat and the studio night stay later.
