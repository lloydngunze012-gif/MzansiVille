# Living world

Design for Claude to review before Claude Code builds it. Not wired. Tone: warm, funny, South African English, for everyone. The campus sits in SunnyBay. Staff names match `docs/world-designer/social.md` where that file already named them.

## Drivers

1. Hoot. The road is not a bench.
2. Side, friend. Wheels coming.
3. Hoot-hoot. Thank you. Move.
4. This is a street, not the Quad.
5. Robot is green. You are not.
6. Left, left — there we go.
7. Photos later. Road now.
8. I am a taxi. You are a statue.
9. Hoot. Bless you for shifting.
10. Coming through. No rush on my side.
11. The rank is that way. The tar is this way.
12. One step, my friend. Just one.
13. Hoot. We all have a lecture.
14. Mirror, signal, you. In that order.
15. That gap is mine. Kindly.
16. Hoot twice. Still here. Still waiting.
17. Campus is that side. You are in my lane.
18. Nice shoes. Wrong place for them.
19. I will wait. I will also hoot.
20. There. Was that so hard? Drive on.

## Ask a friend for food

Shown when a player asks another player to grab a plate.

### Request

- I'm stuck here and starving. Grab me a Caf plate? I'll Woza you.
- If you're already in the queue, add one for me. Legend behaviour.
- Rescue. Food. You. I will pay. I will also owe you a story.

### Accept

- On it. Kota or bunny. Answer fast.
- I was going anyway. Your tray is now my personality.
- Sit. Do not move. I am becoming a delivery person.

### Decline

- Eish, I'm in the library. Rain check, and I mean the food.
- Queue is a country from here. Next slot I got you.
- Can't now. Eat a banana and forgive me.

### Thank you

- You're a legend. I owe you a vetkoek and a quiet week.
- It arrived. I exist again. Thank you.
- Woza sent. Pride also sent. Come eat the chips.
