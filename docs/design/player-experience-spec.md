# MzansiVille: Player Experience Spec

Written from the player's seat, not the builder's. Every feature exists to make a student feel one thing:

**"First year is my fresh start. Nobody here knows who I was. Who am I going to become?"**

Every scene must answer three questions for the player: What do I want right now? What am I choosing? What happens next?

---

## 1. The fantasy: remaking yourself

First year is a reinvention. The game tracks who the player is becoming and shows it back to them.

**Identity paths.** Choices push the player along five paths. They are not stats to grind; they are a reputation that people react to.

- **The Scholar:** lectures, library, tests, Dean's List.
- **The Socialite:** parties, friends, the Quad, being known.
- **The Hustler:** side hustles, money, deals, running a business from res.
- **The Leader:** SRC, societies, standing up for people.
- **The Creative:** open mic, music, fashion, content.

**The player sees the change:**
- A title on the student card that updates: "New kid" becomes "Becoming: The Hustler" becomes "Known as: The Hustler".
- People react: students greet them differently, invitations arrive, a rival notices.
- The glow-up is visible: new outfits, haircuts and room decor unlock and are bought with earned money.
- End of each semester: a "Who you became" reveal comparing who they said they were in high school with who they are now.

## 2. The goal ladder

Goals at every scale, always visible, so the player never wonders "now what?"

| Scale | Example | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Year** | Pass first year, plus one personal dream chosen at the start: Dean's List, win an SRC seat, build a hustle to R5,000, find love, become campus famous | Student card, with a progress bar |
| **Semester** | Qualify for exams (DP), pass all modules, make the team, win the SRC election | Student card |
| **Week** | "Test on Friday: study at least twice", "Pay rent by Saturday" | Phone, Goals tab |
| **Day** | Three goals set every morning by story, schedule and earlier choices | Goal card when the day starts, then a small HUD chip |
| **Right now** | One nudge: "Lecture in 20 min", "Your crush is at the Quad", "You're starving" | HUD chip, top left |

**Rules:**
- Completing any goal gives a visible reward: a sound, a small celebration, money or closeness or a title step.
- Day goals mix one "must" (lecture, rent), one "story" (find out the crush's name) and one "dream" (earn R50 toward the hustle).
- Failing a goal is not game over. It creates story: a missed lecture means a DP warning; unpaid rent means an angry landlord text.

## 3. Storylines that continue

Each arc runs across days and weeks. Every day advances at least two arcs. Every day ends on a cliffhanger from one of them.

1. **The roommate (Zanele or Sipho).** Starts as a stranger, becomes a best friend or a falling-out. Beats: first-night talk, borrowing money, a fight over noise, a secret they share, needing the player's help in week 3.
2. **The crush.** First seen at the bus stop on day 1. The player chooses who they're interested in during creation (guys, girls, everyone, or no romance). Beats: meet-cute at the bus, finding out their name, a text, a study date, jealousy over a rival, asking them out or staying friends.
3. **The rival.** Someone in the same course who competes for marks, the crush, or the SRC seat. Can end as an enemy or an unlikely friend.
4. **Money.** NSFAS delays, rent, data running out, the first side hustle, the first big earning week. Different for NSFAS, bursary and parent-funded students.
5. **The lecturer.** A strict lecturer the player can impress (answer in class, submit on time) or clash with (late, cheeky). Pays off at test time.
6. **The SRC election.** Campaigners at the Quad from week 1, a debate, the player's vote, and possibly running themselves in semester 2.
7. **Home.** Calls from family: pride, pressure, being first in the family at university, money requests. Choices affect mood and money.

## 4. Random daily moments

One to three surprises a day keep every day different. Some are pure flavour; most nudge an arc forward. Each one asks for a choice.

- Bump into your crush at the bus stop, cafeteria queue or library.
- Load shedding mid-lecture, mid-study or mid-cooking.
- A taxi strike: walk, pay for an Uber, or skip the day.
- Free food at a society event.
- Fire alarm at res at 2am.
- Lecture cancelled: sleep in, study, or go to the Quad?
- A surprise test announced.
- Party invite in the res group chat.
- Rain, and you didn't bring an umbrella.
- An NSFAS SMS: good news or bad.
- Data runs out.
- Someone "borrows" your charger.
- You meet someone from your old high school who knew the old you.

## 5. The first 15 minutes, beat by beat

What the player sees, what they choose, and what they feel. Travel and obvious steps are never chores: skip them, or make them a moment.

**0:00 Cover.** Big, stylish, full of energy. Tagline: "New campus. New you. Who will you become?" *Feeling: excited, this is my world.*

**0:30 Creator, part 1: your look.** A full-body character that matches the cover's style. Music plays; the character dances. *Feeling: that's me.*

**1:30 Creator, part 2: your story.**
- How you're funded: NSFAS, bursary or parents. This sets starting money and stress.
- Where you're from: a township, a rural town, a city suburb.
- **"In high school I was..."** (the quiet one, the class clown, the sporty one, the one nobody noticed). **"This year I want to be..."** (one of the five paths).
- Your dream for the year (sets the year goal).
- Who you're interested in.

*Feeling: this is a fresh start, and the game knows what I want.*

**3:00 Morning in res.** The alarm: snooze or get up. The roommate introduces themselves with real personality and asks one question about you. Breakfast choice: noodles, the tuck shop on the way, the cafeteria on campus, or skip. Then "Head to the bus stop?" with Walk (watch) or Skip. *Feeling: quick, personal, my choices.*

**4:30 The bus stop: the meet-cute.** The crush is waiting for the same bus. The bus is late. Three ways to respond: say something funny, ask about the bus, or put on headphones. On the ride: sit next to them or not. *Feeling: oh, a story is starting.*

**5:30 Arrival: today's goals.** A card slides in:
1. Get to your 9:00 lecture.
2. Find out their name. (Only if you didn't ask.)
3. Earn your first R50. (From your dream.)

*Feeling: I know exactly what I'm doing today.*

**6:00 The lecture.** Keep the current scene; it's the best part of the game. Add: the lecturer cold-calls the player with a quick 3-option question (a tiny mini-game), and announces "Test on Friday", which creates the week goal. *Feeling: pressure, and a chance to shine.*

**9:00 After the lecture: the Quad comes alive.** The roommate texts: "Where are you? Come to the Quad." There, the open mic, the SRC campaigners in party colours, Mam' Gugu's stall, and the crush across the Quad with friends. The player can talk to anyone. *Feeling: alive, too many things I want to do.*

**10:30 Choose your afternoon.** One clear choice of how to spend the next block: a hustle (earn R50, the dream goal), the library (the test), or hanging out (Social, the crush). Each option is a short visible activity, not a progress bar. *Feeling: my time is precious, and I'm choosing who I become.*

**13:00 Evening in res.** The roommate wants to talk, or there's a party in the group chat. One choice.

**14:00 Day summary.**
- Goals done (with celebration) and goals missed (with consequence).
- The identity shift: "People are starting to see you as: The Hustler."
- Money, grades, social, closeness with the roommate and the crush.

**14:30 The cliffhanger.** A text arrives on the phone. Examples:
- The crush: "Hey, it's [name] from the bus. You left your earphones on the seat."
- The roommate: "We need to talk tomorrow."
- NSFAS: "Your allowance has been delayed."

**15:00 Tomorrow.** "Day 2 starts when you're ready." Reasons to come back are on screen: an unanswered text, a test on Friday, hustle money waiting.

## 6. Rules for every scene

1. **Choices, not chores.** If a button only does the obvious thing ("Get on the bus"), remove it or skip it. Every tap is a choice or a moment.
2. **People, not menus.** Anything worth doing happens with or near a person who reacts.
3. **Show, don't count.** Every action is visible: walking, queueing, buying, eating, sitting, reading. Numbers come after, briefly.
4. **Every day ends on a cliffhanger.**
5. **Every day changes the player.** Something about who they're becoming moves forward and is shown back to them.
6. **Ten-second test.** If a player could put the phone down for ten seconds and miss nothing, that moment is too slow.

## 7. Build order

1. Goal ladder: the day goal card, the HUD "right now" chip, the year dream picked at creation.
2. Creator part 2: funding, hometown, high-school self, who you want to be, dream, interest.
3. The crush arc, days 1 to 5, starting with the bus-stop meet-cute.
4. Identity paths and the student-card title.
5. Day summary and cliffhanger texts on the phone.
6. Random daily moments deck (start with 10).
7. The other arcs: roommate, rival, money, lecturer, SRC, home.
8. The week: Days 1 to 5 each with a goal, the Friday test as the first big payoff.
